Mental Wellness in Rural Areas

A radio documentary on mental health in the rural Alleghenies, built from interviews with a county behavioral-health official, a therapist and a representative of Project Semicolon.

By Cooper Tretter and Rory Bretz

SLB Radio, 2018 · 6:56

In summer 2018, the Community Foundation for the Alleghenies welcomed ten Youth Philanthropy Interns. This eight-week program was made possible through a grant from The Heinz Endowments and is based on its long-running similar program in Pittsburgh. Throughout their internship, students assessed community needs and developed two opportunities for local nonprofits to apply for grants of up to $25,000 to address these needs. In addition to their grantmaking, interns created radio documentaries with training from SLB Radio Productions in Pittsburgh. SLB aired the features across its 10-station network.

Based on interviews with Tracy Selak (Cambria County Behavioral Health), Jesper Nielsen (Croyle-Nielsen Therapeutic Associates) and Cassidy Klein (Project Semicolon). Series: Inclined to Care: Lifting the Alleghenies to New Heights.
